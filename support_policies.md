Support Team Coverage and Admin Policies
 
Hybrid Work Schedule

	•	Team members are expected to work 4 days per week in the office, while the remaining day is a floating work from home day.
	•	Plan your schedule around your WFH day; use your WFH day when you have an appointment, or when you are expecting a delivery. If you take more than 1 WFH in a week, you will need to make it up on a following week so that there is a total of 4 WFH/month.
	•	If you take more than 1 WFH day in a week, make it up later that month to maintain 4 WFH days per month.
	•	Notify your team slack channel when you plan to wfh (#support-uk/east/west-comms) 
During onboarding: 
You're encouraged to spend extra time in the office to ramp up, ask questions, and connect with the team.
 
Chat Shifts

Our team are responsible for providing live support to customers via chat and are available from 2am - 6pm PT. 
	•	The team schedule can be found in the TSE Hourly Blocks Schedule. The schedule is based on the number of people we need on live chats and office hours throughout the day. 
	•	See Support Shift Expectations for a complete overview of on chat responsibilities. 
	•	Live Chat Schedule: 6 Hours → 5 Hours:After reaching 5 months and consistently meeting efficiency metrics for 4 weeks, a new hire becomes eligible for a reduced 5-hour live chat schedule. The transition depends on timing within the quarter:
	◦	If the 5-month mark falls in the first month of the quarter, the TSE moves to a 5-hour schedule.
	◦	If it falls after the first month, the TSE stays on a 6-hour schedule until the end of the quarter (EOQ).
	•	Punctuality: 
	◦	Be on time — Arriving late means someone may have to cover for you, sacrificing their lunch, meeting time, or office hour
	◦	TSEs must be on live chat at start of shift, whether remote or in-office.
	◦	Do not commute during on chat hours
	◦	Use best judgment for ad-hoc changes and coordinate in #support_logistics if coverage is needed
 
Fallback Schedule (All Hands on Deck)

What is it?
When a region (EMEA or NAMER) doesn’t have enough TSEs to meet chat volume, all TSEs in that region are required to work 6-hour live chat shifts. Fallback schedule is available at go/fallback-schedule
What triggers it?
	•	Managers determine when the fallback schedule is activated
	•	Triggered if >20% of TSEs  are out in a region, in addition to the average we predicted (3 in NAMAR and 1 in UK) 
	•	Fallback may be regional only — e.g., 2 TSEs out in the UK doesn't mean SF needs to adjust
How will I know it's active?
	•	A message will be posted in #support_logistics when fallback is in effect
What if I have an appointment during fallback?
	•	If you need to be off queue for 1 hour or more (i.e. doctor appointments), notify your team and manager in #support_logistics with 1 day advance notice
	•	Appointments must be added to your Google Calendar
	•	Exceptions: Sick days and emergencies
 
Time Off

We encourage you to take time off to recharge and enjoy time with friends and family. Sigma offers a flexible PTO policy that allows employees to take time off as needed as long as they are getting their work done and receive approval from their manager. The technical support team have a critical customer facing role so in order to sustain adequate staffing levels and our ability to maintain a high quality customer experience, the following guidelines will be observed.
Sick leave Policy and Official Holidays can be found in the All-Company policy. 
PTO Procedures

PTO requests should ideally be reviewed with your manager a minimum of 2 weeks before the date of PTO. It’s important to discuss with your manager as soon as possible if the needed date(s) will occur sooner than a two week timeframe.
Submitting a PTO Request
	1	Use the PTO Request Workflow in the #support_logistics Slack channel for manager approval (Note: the return date should the next business day that you will be back at work, the additional details field is optional but please consider leaving a comment for time off requests with less than two weeks notice)
	2	Once approved, submit the request in ADP (US)/PeopleHR (UK)
	3	Add your PTO days to the Sigma Office Calendar
	4	Block your Google calendar with an all day Out of Office event (Note: this is required to prevent customers from booking Office Hours appointments on your schedule while you’re away)
PTO Request Guidelines 
	•	Submit your PTO at least 2 weeks in advance 
	•	Maximum number of TSEs on PTO each day is limited to 1 TSE in UK and 2 TSEs in NY & SF
	•	 Make sure to review the PTO schedule before putting your request in 
	◦	Exceptions to the limit can be made on a case by case basis; these are reviewed by the respective support manager
	◦	For non-major holidays exceptions will be made, where 5-7 TSEs (limit of 3 per NAMAR region) can be on PTO/Opt out (dependent on the holiday)
	•	On-call coverage: Review Incident.io schedule before submitting PTO requests. If scheduled for on-call, you are responsible for arranging coverage by swapping with another TSE and updating the schedule in incident.io. You should make sure you have coverage before this request is approved.

	▪	Document the situation in internal notes.
	▪	Leave a clear note explaining the status (e.g., “Out on PTO, unable to complete before handoff”).
	▪	Where feasible, notify the customer that reassignment will occur.

Unplanned Sick Leave Procedures

Keeping your work visible and urgent issues flagged ensures the team succeeds and customers never get left behind. In the event of short-term absence (1–3 days), the following steps should be followed. If the absence becomes extended you should coordinate with your manager to ensure all active or urgent tasks are fully reassigned and appropriate coverage is arranged.
Communicate your absence with your manager and team:
	•	Notify your manager as soon as possible and ensure your time off is acknowledged
	•	Notify the team in #support-logistics
	•	Update your Slack status to 🤒 Off Sick 
	•	Set yourself to Unavailable in Calendly for your OOO dates (if you take Office Hours)
	•	US ONLY - add sick leave in ADP
Manage and handover your active queue: 
It is your responsibility to ensure continuity of customer support for tickets you own before going offline:
	1	Enable re-assign replies in Intercom
	2	Review your active chats and leave chat summaries for any ongoing chats to aid handover (Note: You can use AI compose to generate a summary)
	3	If a ticket requires immediate attention, un-assign yourself to drop the ticket back into the resolution queue for pick up, mark it as a priority ticket and tag your manager to ensure a timely handover. (Note: Do not un-assign the ticket from resolution team as this will leave the ticket in a limbo state)
Office Hours: 
If you have an office hours scheduled it is your responsibility to either find coverage or notify the customer you are unavailable and provide them with a link to re-schedule the meeting. Given office hours availability can be hard to come by you should prioritise finding coverage where possible. 
On-Call Coverage:
	•	If you are scheduled for on-call, you are responsible for arranging coverage by swapping with another TSE or scheduling an override in Incident.io  before your absence.
	•	Notify your manager and update the on-call schedule once coverage is secured.
Manager Responsibilities
	•	Update the hourly blocks schedule
	•	Review the TSEs queue to ensure necessary chat and task reassignments are handled promptly
	•	Ensure coverage of on-call duties if a TSE is On-Call and out
	•	UK ONLY - Input the TSEs sick leave in PeopleHR 
Holiday Coverage 

Sigma observes a number of Holidays throughout the year. Please refer to the Sigma Observed Holidays list in confluence. 
Throughout the calendar year, Sigma observes several dates that may or may not be observed by all of our customers or in all parts of the world. To ensure adequate support presence, the Support team has set minimum staffing targets for these days. 
Support staffing on all holidays is determined on a case by case basis. Support leadership looks at both the demand of support in the weeks leading up to the date and the seasonality of the holiday. 
All non-major holidays are opt-out; meaning you are required to submit a time off request for that day if you wish to take it off.  
Non-Major Holidays

Non-Major holidays are any holiday Sigma observes outside of; Thanksgiving, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year’s Day.
Submission of PTO request is required for non-major holidays: All support staff planning to take off on these days must submit a PTO request; otherwise, they are assumed to be working. 
Team members working non-major holidays are compensated with an alternative day off of the observed holiday that they worked on. Where possible we request this day is taken within +- 7 days of the observed holiday. To book this in, you should follow the PTO workflow on #support_logistics after first checking availability in the Time-off Visuals workbook. 
Major Holidays

Major Holidays are defined as Thanksgiving, Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Years. During these holidays the Support department, as well as the rest of the Sigma office, is closed. 
The Intercom Special Notice will be updated to notify customers about limited support presence. The following template will be used to update the special notice:
Sigma Support will be out of the office on <date> in observance of <holiday>. The team will return on <date>. For any urgent issues, reach out to support@sigmacomputing.com.
Office hours will be adjusted to reflect the days off and reverted to the default schedule when the team returns. For example if we have Monday off, adjust the schedule so that office hours is on Tuesday, 6-6pm.
Thanksgiving and Day After Thanksgiving

The office is closed on Thanksgiving Day (The fourth Thursday in November which will be Thursday, 11/22/in 2024 and Thursday 11/27 in 2025) and the Day After Thanksgiving. 
For the week leading up to Thanksgiving day it is business as usual and the normal PTO guidelines will apply. 
Christmas Day and New Years Day

The office is closed on Christmas Day (Dec. 25th) and New Years Day (Jan 1st). 
The two weeks spanning these two dates (essentially between the 12/21/2025 and 1/2/2025 - referred to as the “Holiday Break”) will see the Sigma office operating in a reduced capacity and this reflects business activity in both the US and other parts of the world during this time.
During the Holiday Break, support staff will also operate at a reduced capacity.  For the days between 12/21/2025 and 1/2/2025, support leadership will have an increased capacity to approve time off requests. These will be reviewed on a first-come first-served basis and will be approved at support leadership’s discretion. The support leadership team measures capacity for the week based on the support volume that is handled immediately before the holiday season, and the cadence of median daily chat volume during normal operating days of Q4.  
If you do not request specific days off during the Holiday Break, you are assumed to be working. The support leadership team will make an effort to accommodate every request possible but there are limits on the number of requests that can be approved. 
During the Holiday Break all hourly blocks that are not on-chat or lunch will be treated as off-chat. 
Holiday adjacency

On Sigma Observed Holidays when the majority of the team is off, you may want to take additional days off either before or after. To ensure we have enough coverage, we will handle these as first-come first-serve (based on the guidelines above) and are approved at the discretion of the Support Management team, so please make sure any requests are submitted in a timely manner.
Support On Call coverage during the holidays 

All Support on-call schedules in Incident.io are applicable for all Sigma Observed Holidays and there is no change to how these days are handled. If you have requested time off check the incident.io schedule to determine if you are on-call for any time off requested. Before the PTO request is submitted you should find another TSE to swap or cover for the period of PTO that overlaps with the period that you are on-call.
Wishing us all a great holiday season with maximum family and friends time, personal time to recharge, and hopefully minimal SEVs!
